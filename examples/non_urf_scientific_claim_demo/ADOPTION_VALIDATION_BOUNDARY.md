# Adoption / Validation Boundary

## ADOPTION_SIGNALS

The following are adoption signals only:

- clone
- fork
- dependency use
- issue reference
- independent run of the documented verifier or test

These signals may show that the artifact was found, copied, inspected, or executed.

## NOT_SCIENTIFIC_VALIDATION

Adoption signals do not constitute scientific validation.

They do not prove Ohm's Law, validate a physical instrument, validate the broader URF research program, or provide peer review.

## SCIENTIFIC_VALIDATION

Scientific validation is not claimed here.

Scientific validation would require independent domain-appropriate review of the relevant mathematical or scientific content, not merely successful execution of this fixed-observation demo.
